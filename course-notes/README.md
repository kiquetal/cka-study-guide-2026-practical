# Course Notes

A **free-form** space for your own notes. There is no rigid chapter/module structure
on purpose — write however works for you while watching the O'Reilly video or reading
the book.

## Files

- **[`notes.md`](notes.md)** — your running notes. Dump whatever you want here:
  commands, "aha" moments, screenshots of concepts, gotchas. Date your entries so you
  can track progress over time.
- **[`questions-for-kiro.md`](questions-for-kiro.md)** — a queue of things you want me
  (Kiro) to research or explain later. When you hit something you don't want to break
  your flow on, jot it here and I'll pick it up.

## Primary resources

- **PRIMARY:** _CKA Study Guide, 2nd Edition_ (Benjamin Muschko) — 2025 overhaul edition.
- **SECONDARY:** _CKA, 4th Edition_ [Video].

## Tip

Later, as you review, distill the most useful notes into the matching domain folder
under [`../domains/`](../domains/) so they double as exam cheatsheets.
